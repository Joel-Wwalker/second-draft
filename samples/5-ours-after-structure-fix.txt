Helen of Troy is a really famous figure in Greek mythology. People celebrate her for being extraordinarily beautiful and for her involvement in the Trojan War. She was the daughter of Zeus and married Menelaus, who was the king of Sparta. Then, the Trojan prince Paris took her to Troy, which then sparked a Greek expedition to retrieve her. That expedition ended up becoming a war that lasted for ten years. More than anyone else in the story, Helen is blamed for the war.
