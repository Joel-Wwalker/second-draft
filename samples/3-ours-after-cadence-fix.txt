Helen of Troy is one of the most famous figures in Greek mythology. She is known for her extraordinary beauty and for her role in starting the Trojan War. She married Menelaus, the king of Sparta, but then she left with, or was taken by, the Trojan prince Paris, and they went to Troy. Menelaus and other Greek leaders responded by launching a large expedition to bring her back. This began a war that lasted for ten years. While Helen is often blamed for the conflict, many versions of the myth show her as a complicated person. The gods, fate, and the powerful men around her all influenced her decisions.
