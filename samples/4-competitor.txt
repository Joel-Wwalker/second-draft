Helen of Troy one of the most iconic characters from Greek mythology is a woman who is known for her exceptional beauty and her responsibility for the outbreak of the Trojan War. As the wife of Menelaus, the King of Sparta, she was captured by or ran away with the beautiful Trojan prince Paris who led her to Troy; this caused Menelaus and other notable leaders to launch an elaborate and large campaign against Trojans to retrieve her, setting in motion an epic ten-year conflict. Even though Helen has mostly been seen as responsible for the war, many of the stories about her have been changing as time has passed where she turns out to be a character who is caught between a web of gods, fate and the powerful men in her environment.
